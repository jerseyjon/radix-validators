About the 15% validator fee

All three validators are set to 15%. DIFD4 charges it already, because a fee set at creation applies immediately. For Radix Charts V2 and DoItForDan it is an increase, so it does not take effect until epoch 345,107 and 345,108 — around 29 September 2026. Until then those two still charge 2.5% and 5% respectively.

It is worth being straight about why the fee rose: hosting costs rose sharply during 2026 — one provider raised cloud instance prices by up to 3.1× in June alone — while the XRD price fell, so the same emissions cover much less of the running cost. Most of the validator set has moved into the 10–20% range for the same reason.

Radix delays any fee increase by 4,032 epochs, roughly two weeks, so you always have notice and time to move your stake. A fee decrease applies at the next epoch.

Stake and uptime figures are deliberately not repeated here — read them from the Radix Dashboard, which is authoritative for live values. Assets in github.com/jerseyjon/radix-validators.
